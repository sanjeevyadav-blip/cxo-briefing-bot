  • RBI allows Sebi-regulated depositories to include customer deposit details in statements: What changes for investors? Economic Times

    The Reserve Bank of India announced that, effective by December 31 2026, SEBI‑registered depositories will be permitted to add bank deposit information to the consolidated account statements (CAS) they provide to investors. This change will allow holders of demat accounts to see details of their bank deposits alongside securities such as equities and bonds in a single statement. The RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra communicated the decision while delivering the bi‑monthly monetary policy announcement.

  • RBI breaks 4-year rate-hike drought Economic Times

    On Wednesday, the RBI’s Monetary Policy Committee raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50%, marking the first increase in almost four years. The six‑member panel voted unanimously and shifted its policy stance from neutral to “calibrated tightening,” indicating that further hikes could be considered. The move was driven by rising oil prices linked to US‑Iran tensions and a weak monsoon, which together threatened higher inflation. The previous hike had been in February 2023, when the rate moved to 6.50%.
