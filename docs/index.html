  • Biscuit maker Anmol Industries files for $188 million IPO Economic Times

    Anmol Industries, a Kolkata‑based biscuit maker founded in 1994, has filed a draft prospectus for an initial public offering of up to 18 billion rupees (approximately $187.9 million). The Baijnath Choudhary & Family Trust, which holds about 84 % of Anmol, is the sole selling shareholder; the company itself is not issuing new shares and will not receive any proceeds. Anmol markets biscuits, cookies and cakes under 70 brands and exports to 31 countries. For the fiscal year ending 31 March, the firm reported a net profit of 1.91 billion rupees, a five‑fold increase from the prior year, while revenue also rose.
