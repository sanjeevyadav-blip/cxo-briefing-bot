  • RBI net buyer of dollars for fourth straight month in September, data shows Economic Times

    The Reserve Bank of India was a net buyer of foreign currency in September, marking its fourth consecutive month of net purchases. According to the RBI’s monthly bulletin, the central bank bought a net $6.94 billion, with gross purchases of $82.18 billion and sales of $75.25 billion. In August, the RBI had bought a net $43.26 billion. The continued buying is linked to interventions, swap maturities and the absorption of capital inflows, including about $143 billion from a zero‑cost hedging facility for banks’ overseas FX deposits.

  • Rising dependence of NBFCs on bank borrowings needs to be monitored on an ongoing basis: RBI bulletin BusinessLine

    The RBI’s bulletin notes that bank borrowings dominate funding for non‑bank financial companies (NBFCs), especially for micro‑finance institutions (NBFC‑MFIs) and Investment and Credit Companies (NBFC‑ICCs). This indicates a strong reliance on banks by retail‑focused and micro‑finance‑oriented NBFCs. The bulletin recommends ongoing monitoring of this dependence to assess financial stability risks.

  • US inflation rises to 3.8% in October, nearly double the Fed’s 2% target BusinessLine

    U.S. consumer price inflation rose to 3.8 % in October, nearly double the Federal Reserve’s 2 % target. The data show that while monthly price pressures moderated, inflation‑adjusted consumption weakened. At the same time, personal income and household savings increased, suggesting mixed signals for the economy and monetary policy.

These steps are aimed at improving profitability and operational efficiency across the Air India group.
