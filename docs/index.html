  • RBI grants Core Investment Company registration to Indiabulls BusinessLine

    The Reserve Bank of India has approved a Core Investment Company (CIC) registration for Indiabulls. A CIC is a non‑bank financial company that mainly holds investments in, and provides funding to, entities within its own corporate group, rather than engaging in traditional lending or other financial activities. This registration allows Indiabulls to operate under the regulatory framework applicable to CICs, focusing on intra‑group investments and capital support.
