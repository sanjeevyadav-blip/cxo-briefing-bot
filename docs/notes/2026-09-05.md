## [Central banks change where they stash gold](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/policy/gold-reserves-are-on-the-move-but-central-banks-are-choosing-different-vaults/articleshow/133788083.cms) -- Economic Times
Central banks are relocating gold reserves. The Netherlands moved about 86 tonnes from the United States and Canada to London, citing crisis preparedness and easier market access. France shifted 129 tonnes from New York to Europe because its New York‑held bars did not meet required purity standards. India’s latest data show that roughly 77 % of its gold is now held domestically, indicating a rise in internal holdings, though the Reserve Bank of India has not provided further comment.
